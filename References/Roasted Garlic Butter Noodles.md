## Ingredients
- 1 Pack of Spaghetti noodles
- 2 Bulbs of garlic, pealed
- 1/4 cup of butter
- 1tbsp of tomato paste
- 3tbsp parmesan cheese
- 3 basil leaves
- 1 cup of olive oil
- Red Pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. In a pot, cook spaghetti according to package instructions. Reserve some pasta water before draining.
2. In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium-low heat. Add the peeled garlic and cook for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the garlic is golden brown. Then, remove the garlic and drain the oil.
3. In a blender, combine the cooked garlic, butter, tomato paste, basil leaves, parmesan cheese , and 2 tablespoons of reserved pasta water. Add some black pepper if desired. Blend until smooth.
4. Transfer the cooked pasta to the large pan. Add the garlic butter mixture and toss until the pasta is evenly coated.
5. If the sauce is too thick, gradually add more reserved pasta water until you reach your desired texture.
6. Add Red Pepper to taste (if desired)
