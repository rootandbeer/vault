## Description

In a novel set in an indefinite, futuristic, post-apocalyptic world, a father and his young son make their way through the ruins of a devastated American landscape, struggling to survive and preserve the last remnants of their own humanity

## Notes
I really enjoyed the book, though I felt the ending came to fast.

I did like how to book was written. There wasn't any information given on how the apocalypse happened, you were just thrown right in the middle of the story of the father and son's journey trying to survive. The other interesting part was that there were no names given to any of the characters. I felt this added a lot to the book and added to the impersonal vibe of the story.
