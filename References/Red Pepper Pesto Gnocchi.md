## Ingredients
- 1 Bag of non-frozen Gnocchi. [Trader Joes Gnocchi](https://www.traderjoes.com/home/products/pdp/gnocchi-073578)
- 12oz of Spicy Italian Chicken Sausage
- 1 Jar of Roasted Red Pepper & Almond Pesto Sauce
- Parmesan Cheese, to taste
- 3-4 chopped Basil Leaves (optional)

## Instructions
1. Slice chicken sausage into bite-sized pieces
2. In a large pan heat olive oil over medium heat. Add gnocchi and sausage and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi and sausage start to brown. Salt if necessary.
3. Pour in pest sauce and stir until the gnocchi and sausage are evenly coated.
4. Add in several tablespoons of parmesan cheese and mix well.
5. Once everything is heated through and well combined, add 3-4 chopped basil leaves.
