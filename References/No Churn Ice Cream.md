## All Recipes
All recipes use a 32oz Tub of Zero Sugar Cool Whip.
## Pineapple and Toasted Coconut
### Ingredients
- 14oz Can of Sweetened Condensed Milk
- 1/2 Cup of Crushed Pineapple
- 1/2 Cup of Toasted Coconut
## Rocky Road
### Ingredients
- 1 pack of Jello Chocolate Fudge Pudding
- 1/2 Cup of Toasted Coconut
- 1/2 Cup of Marshmallows
- 1/2 Cup of Chocolate Chips or Nuts
## Directions
- Thaw Zero Sugar Cool Whip on the counter for about 2 hours or until soft enough to mix
- Add base ingredient (sweetened condensed milk, jelly pack, etc...) and mix thoroughly for several minutes to make sure its incorporated
- Add all other ingredients and mix
- Put in freezer for 4+ hours until frozen

## Notes

-  Ingredients can be halved for smaller batches
