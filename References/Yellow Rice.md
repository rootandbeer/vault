## Ingredients
- 2 c Rice
- 4 c Chicken Broth
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 Diced bell pepper
- 1 tsp Turmeric
- 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1/4 tsp Paprika
- 1/4 tsp Chili Powder

## Instructions
1. In a pan melt butter and add rice and bell pepper coating stirring occasionally until rice starts to brown.
2. Add in chicken broth, turmeric, garlic powder, paprika and chili powder. Bring to boil then cover, reduce heat, and let simmer for 20 minutes.
