## Ingredients
- 1 Onion
- 2 boxes of Creamy Tomato Soup
- 1 lb ground beef
- Spinach
- ~20oz pack cheese Tortellini
- 1 tsp Taco seasoning
- Sour cream
- Mozzarella

## Instructions
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat, add the **diced onion**. Sauté for **3-4 minutes** until translucent.
2. Add the **ground beef** & taco seasoning. Cook until its **browned and fully cooked** (5-7 minutes). Drain excess fat if needed.
3. Pour in both boxes of **creamy tomato soup**. Stir to combine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom for extra flavor.
4. Bring to a **gentle boil** then add the **cheese tortellini** directly into the soup. Reduce to a simmer and cook according to package instructions (usually **5-8 minutes**) until tortellini are tender.
5. **Finish with Spinach & Sour Cream** and let it simmer for 1-2 minutes
6. Top with mozzarella cheese
