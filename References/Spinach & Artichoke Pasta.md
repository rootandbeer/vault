## Ingredients
- 1 (8 oz) Frozen box of Spinach & Artichoke Dip from Trader Joes
- 1 1lb of Garlic and Herb susage
- 1 (16 oz) Pack of bowtie (farfalle) pasta
- Grated Parmesan cheese  (to taste)
- Olive Oil

## Instructions
1. Cook bowtie pasta according to packaging. Before draining, reserve some pasta water for later.
2. While pasta cooks,  slice the sausage and saute in a large pan w/ oil over medium heat until brown.
3. Heat up Spinach & Artichoke dip according to package instructions.
4. Once pasta has been cooked and drained, add it to the pan with the sausage.
5. Pour in warmed Spinach & Artichoke dip and stir to combine
6. Gradually add 2-4 tablespoons of the reserved pasta water, stiring until the sauce reaches your desired consistency
7. Sprinkle in a few small handfuls of grated Parmesan cheese and mix until melted and well incorporated.
8. Server warm, optionally top with extra Parmesan.
