## Ingredients
- 1 Gallon of Lemonade
- 1 Bottle 1800 Coconut Tequila
- 1 Cup Triple Sec
- 1/2 Cup Lime Juice

## Directions
- Pour out about 5 cups of lemonade from the gallon jug into a separate container.
- Add the entire bottle of 1800 Coconut Tequila, the Cointreau (or Triple Sec), and the lime juice directly into the gallon jug.
- Secure the cap tightly and shake the jug thoroughly until everything is well combined.
- Refrigerate until chilled, then serve over ice. For an elevated summer touch, garnish with a lime wedge or rim glasses with sugar or salt.

## Notes
- Recipe can be halved
