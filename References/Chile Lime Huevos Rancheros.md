## Ingredients
- 3 tablespoons Plain Whole Milk Greek Yogurt
- ¼ teaspoon TJ’s Chile Lime Seasoning Blend
- 1 teaspoon fresh juice from 1 Lime
- Olive Oil (for pan, about 1-2 tablespoons)
- 2 Large Eggs
- TJ’s Chile Lime Seasoning Blend, to taste
- 2 Tortillas
- 2 tablespoons TJ’s Cowboy Caviar
- Cotija Cheese, crumbled, to taste (or use Mexican cheese)

## Instructions
1. Make the crema:
    In a small bowl, combine yogurt, ¼ teaspoon Chile Lime Seasoning, and lime juice. Mix well and set aside.
2. Cook eggs sunny side up in oil
3. Warm the tortillas in a pan
4. Add the Cowboy Caviar to the same skillet and heat briefly, just until warm.
5. Place tortillas on a plate. Top with eggs, then add Cowboy Caviar. Spoon crema over the top, sprinkle with Cotija cheese, and add a lime wedge. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and additional Chile Lime Seasoning, as desired. Serve immediately and enjoy!
