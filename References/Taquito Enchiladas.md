## Ingredients
- 1 package TJ's Taquitos (Beef or Chicken)
- 1 jar Enchilada Sauce
- 2 cups Shredded Mexican Cheese Blend, divided
- 1/2 can Sliced Black Olives
- 3 Green Onions, white and green parts, sliced
- 1/2 can Fire Roasted Diced Green Chiles
## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Arrange taquitos in a single layer in a 7-inch by 10-inch baking dish and cover evenly with enchilada sauce. Top with 1 1/2 cups cheese, followed by olives and chiles. 
    
2. Bake casserole on center rack of oven for 20 to 25 minutes, or until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Remove pan from oven and garnish with remaining cheese and green onions. Allow to cool slightly before serving.
