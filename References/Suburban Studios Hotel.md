Hotel is by the airport but only a short drive to downtown SLC (~15 mins). Hotel had plenty of amenities for the value (we paid about $80/night) such as:
- Outdoor Pool
- Washer & Dryer room
- Gym
- Free Breakfast
- Attached bar/restaurant
- Inside the room had stove and full fridge.
