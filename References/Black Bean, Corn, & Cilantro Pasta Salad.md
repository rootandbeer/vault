## Ingredients
- 1 Red Onion
- 2 Limes, Juiced 
- 16oz of Farafalle / Bowtie Pasta
- 2 Cans Black Beans, Drained & Rinsed
- 1 Bag TJ's Oraganic Super Sweet Cut Corn, thawed
- 1 Cup Cotija Cheese, crumbled
- 1 Cup Cilantro Salad Dressing
- Chopped Cilantro (optional)

## Instructions
1. Cook Bowtie pasta according to instructions
   
2. Combine diced onion and lime juice in a small bowl. Let sit 5-10 minutes while you prepare the salad.  

3. To a large serving bowl, add pasta, black beans, corn, cotija, diced onions and lime juice, 1 cup of dressing, and toss everything together to combine. Season with salt to taste. Garnish with cilantro to serve. If serving cold, refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
