## Description

#1 NEW YORK TIMES BESTSELLER • One of the most remarkable true-crime narratives of the twenty-first century • “The Art Thief, like its title character, has confidence, élan, and a great sense of timing."—The New Yorker A BEST BOOK OF THE YEAR: The Washington Post, The New Yorker, Lit Hub "Enthralling." —The Wall Street Journal Stéphane Bréitwieser is the most prolific art thief of all time. He pulled off more than 200 heists, often in crowded museums in broad daylight. His girlfriend served as his accomplice. His collection was worth an estimated $2 billion. He never sold a piece, displaying his stolen art in his attic bedroom. He felt like a king. Until everything came to a shocking end. In this spellbinding portrait of obsession and flawed genius, Michael Finkel gives us one of the most remarkable true-crime narratives of our times, a riveting story of art, theft, love, and an insatiable hunger to possess beauty at any cost.

## Notes

This book is hard to rate. The story is true and really fascinating, but it's told in such a ...drab... way that it doesn't highlight anything of interest. A lot of what the author talks about is the psychology behind thieves like this, citing studies, and quotes. This book would have been far more interesting if it got into how the items were stolen by Stephane Breitwieser, but instead the descriptions were very surface level.

The book started explaining Stephane Breitwieser as an art aficionado, almost as someone who is romantically or intimately attached to art. It talks about him in a light as almost a hero "freeing" these artworks from the museums as if they were kept hostage. You couldn't help but feel its just done by compulsion and narcissism/sociopathic behavior and that the author is just missing it.

Towards the end of the book the author starts to highlight Breitwieser in a different light, highlighting that he cannot stop stealing even after being given lenient sentences. He is caught multiple times afterwards, and ends up isolating himself as the people around him realize that he is just using them for his own self gratification.

If this book was written better, it could easily be a 4.5 rating, but the parts you want to hear most about are left out.

## Annotations

> Something that a thief would obviously never do is precisely what a thief should consider doing

To me this quote is about stepping outside of your box or comfort. Trying something different or looking at it from a different angle.

This could also mean to be unpredictable. 

> When the quest outshines the treasure, you don't want to stop questing.

Sometimes the high or thrill comes from the action, not the result. It's important to identify which is driving the thrill to not get caught up in the wrong result(s) that may have negative affects.
